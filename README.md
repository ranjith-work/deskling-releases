# Deskling releases

Public downloads for [Deskling](https://deskling.app), the little companion that lives on your Mac desktop.

**Download the latest version:** [deskling.app/get](https://deskling.app/get), or grab `Deskling.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ranjith-work/deskling-releases/releases/latest).

## Requirements

- macOS 12 (Monterey) or later
- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)

## Opening Deskling the first time

Deskling isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time you open it:

1. Open `Deskling.dmg` and drag Deskling into Applications.
2. Open Deskling. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Deskling, then confirm.

You only need to do this once.
